MINI FARM TYCOON — FACEBOOK INSTANT GAMES STARTER PACKAGE

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- index.html: the current playable HTML5 farm prototype, with a basic Facebook Instant Games SDK bootstrap.
- The game is free to test in a normal browser.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- This is a starter/test build, not a guarantee of Facebook approval or public launch.
- Live ads are NOT integrated. The daily bonus is an in-game simulated reward, not an advertisement.
- No real-money payments, cloud saves, accounts, or server backend are connected.
- Publishing and ad monetization depend on Meta account/app eligibility, review, policy compliance, and any current partner requirements. Revenue is not guaranteed.

PUBLISHING STEPS
1. Sign in to Meta for Developers with your own account: https://developers.facebook.com/
2. Create an app and add/configure the Instant Games product if it is available to your account.
3. Open Instant Games > Web Hosting and upload a ZIP containing index.html at the ZIP root.
4. Test the hosted build using the dashboard's test/production controls.
5. Complete all requested app details, business/account checks, privacy disclosures, and reviews. Submit for review only when the build is complete.
6. After the game is approved and launch is enabled, follow Meta's current Games Ads/monetization setup and approval process before expecting ad revenue.
